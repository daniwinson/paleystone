Shady Canyon Country Club, Irvine

Service

Product Defect Investigation

Irvine, CA

Shady Canyon Country Club has engaged PALEY across multiple engagements, including a product defect investigation of the club's stone and tile installations and ongoing maintenance and repair consultation.

PALEY also developed maintenance specifications for the club and trained its staff on their implementation — protecting the installation for the long term rather than just addressing the immediate issue.

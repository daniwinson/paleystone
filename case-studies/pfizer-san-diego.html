Pfizer, San Diego

Service

Installation Analysis & Restoration

Location

San Diego, CA

PALEY Stone & Tile Forensics was contracted to determine the causes of various installation concerns and provide the necessary specifications to restore and repair the stone. The clients realized that the condition and presentation of the stone and installation were a reflection of their company.

After assessing the situation and proposing the needed steps to mitigate the problems, the corrections were implemented. After more than four years, bonding issues have been successfully rectified.

  • Scheduled groundskeeper maintenance
  • Product defect and material unsuitability issues
  • Installation issues including substructure preparation
  • Environmental damage from exposure, water, and pollution

Project Summary

Issues: Bonding & installation concerns
Work: Assessment, restoration & repair specs
Outcome: Bonding issues rectified — 4+ years
Location: San Diego, CA

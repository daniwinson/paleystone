North American College, Houston

Service

Installation & Material Defect Analysis

Location

Houston, TX

Engineered and manufactured materials like quartz, marble, and terrazzo are increasingly desirable, especially in commercial and government buildings, due to LEED credits, greater durability, and perceived lower maintenance needs. Yet these materials have specific installation and maintenance requirements — and if those requirements are not taken into account, serious issues may arise.

  • Follow the manufacturer's installation recommendations
  • Specially modified mortar and/or thinset are required for proper bonding
  • Even though these products are more durable, maintenance is still required
  • Some products can react negatively to sealers; consult the manufacturer for recommendations
